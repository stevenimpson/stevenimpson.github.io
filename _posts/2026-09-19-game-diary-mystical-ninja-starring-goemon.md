Friends had sung the praises of this odd little game for years, so I was convinced to try out Goemon via the PC recomp.

It’s full of personality, even minor townsfolk are often memorable. I enjoyed the fairly leisurely dungeons and rudimentary combat, all in service of a silly fun cartoon story that I could never quite predict.

It’s a good time!
